# AI Usage Statement

## AI Feature In The Game

The main AI design in `Beyond Safe Zone` is Qimian, a hidden protagonist controlled by an AI decision layer.

During the first run, the player controls Chen Xing and sees only the consequences of Qimian's actions. Qimian wakes on day 11 and begins acting on the same shared map, affecting resources, zombie density, routes, anonymous supplies, and blood moon outcomes.

At the end of the demo, Qimian's action log reveals the hidden causes behind events the player experienced.

## Why This Matters

The AI is not a decorative chatbot. It is part of the simulation:

- It has constrained action choices.
- It modifies shared world state.
- It creates mystery during the first run.
- It becomes explainable through a post-demo log.
- It supports replay by turning "random events" into traceable hidden intent.

## AI Tool Usage During Development

Development may use AI tools such as CodeBuddy or Codex for:

- Planning the demo scope and submission materials.
- Drafting gameplay systems and documentation.
- Implementing or reviewing Godot scripts.
- Creating tests and debugging simulation behavior.
- Organizing AI usage records, pitch copy, and video scripts.

AI-generated or AI-assisted content should be reviewed by the developer before submission.

## Evidence To Keep

- CodeBuddy/Codex conversation summaries.
- Notes showing which systems were AI-assisted.
- Before/after documentation changes.
- Test results for AI-related simulation behavior.
- Asset workflow records if AI tools or FrameRonin are used.
