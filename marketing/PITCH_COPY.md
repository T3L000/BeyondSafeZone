# Pitch Copy

## One-Sentence Pitch

`Beyond Safe Zone` is a 2D pixel-art survival management game where the player survives as Chen Xing while an AI-controlled hidden protagonist, Qimian, secretly changes the same city.

## Short Pitch

In 《保护区之外》, the player controls Chen Xing, a normal survivor trying to reach the safe zone through daytime exploration and nighttime shelter management. But the world is not static. Qimian, another protagonist controlled by AI, wakes on day 11 and acts in the same city outside the player's view. The player first sees only the consequences: missing supplies, shifted zombie groups, anonymous medicine, and strange changes before blood moon nights. At the end of the demo, Qimian's action log reveals the hidden cause behind those events.

## Long Pitch

《保护区之外》 is a 2D pixel-art survival management demo built around a visible survival story and a hidden AI-driven story.

The player plays Chen Xing, a normal survivor outside the safe zone. Each day, Chen Xing explores by bicycle, gathers food, water, medicine, parts, and clues, then returns at night to manage the shelter. Day 7 and day 14 bring blood moon events that test the player's preparation.

The innovation is that another protagonist is also alive in the same world. Qimian is infected but conscious, wakes on day 11, and is controlled by an AI decision layer. Qimian can move resources, redirect zombies, help survivors, leave anonymous supplies, and change route danger. The player does not see those actions directly during the first run, but the consequences are real.

When the demo ends, Qimian's hidden action log is revealed. Events that seemed random become traceable decisions made by another character. The AI is therefore not a chat window or content generator; it is a hidden protagonist whose choices reshape the player's survival experience.
