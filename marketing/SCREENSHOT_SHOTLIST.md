# Screenshot And Recording Shot List

Use this checklist when capturing material for submissions, PPT, or demo video.

## Required Shots

- Title or opening screen showing project name.
- Daytime exploration location selection.
- Resource state or inventory pressure.
- Night shelter management view.
- Blood moon warning before day 7 or day 14.
- Blood moon resolution or aftermath.
- A location affected by Qimian, such as missing supermarket supplies or reduced zombie density.
- Anonymous aid or clue from Qimian.
- Demo-end Qimian hidden action log.

## Optional Shots

- Bicycle range or repair state.
- Protection zone broadcast or clue.
- Shelter damage after blood moon.
- Any UI panel that clearly shows day, phase, resources, and danger.

## Capture Rules

- Capture only features that exist in the current build.
- If a shot represents planned behavior, label it as concept or future scope.
- Prefer clear subtitles or callouts for the AI hidden protagonist mechanic.
