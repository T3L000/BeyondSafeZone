# Submission Plan

This is the master contest material plan for 《保护区之外》 / `Beyond Safe Zone`.

## Submission Positioning

`Beyond Safe Zone` is a 2D pixel-art survival management demo about a visible survivor and a hidden AI-controlled protagonist sharing one collapsing city.

The player controls Chen Xing, a normal survivor trying to reach the safe zone. The AI controls Qimian, a hidden infected survivor who wakes on day 11 and secretly changes the shared map by moving resources, redirecting zombies, leaving supplies, and helping people outside the player's view.

## Core Judge Takeaway

The AI feature is part of the game rules and narrative structure:

- AI has a role: Qimian, the hidden protagonist.
- AI has constraints: personality, available actions, world state, and traceable consequences.
- AI changes gameplay: resources, routes, zombie density, anonymous supplies, and blood moon pressure.
- AI creates replay value: the demo-end action log reveals the hidden causes behind the player's first run.

## Current Demo Truth

Implemented or planned for the current contest demo scope:

- Chen Xing line covers the first 14 days.
- Daytime exploration and nighttime shelter management form the core loop.
- Day 7 and day 14 are blood moon pressure points.
- Qimian is hidden during the first run and wakes on day 11.
- Qimian's hidden actions affect shared map state.
- Demo end reveals Qimian's action log.

Do not claim full 30-day gameplay, full playable Qimian route, complete art, or full multi-ending structure unless those are implemented later.
